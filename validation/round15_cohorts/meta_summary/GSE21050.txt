TITLE: Expression data from Complex genetics sarcomas (cohort 1 and 2)
SUMMARY: For this study, we selected, from the French Sarcoma Group (FSG) database, soft tissue sarcomas with no recurrent chromosomal translocations and for which a frozen tissue of the untreated primary tumor was available. Three hundred and ten sarcomas  have been studied. They are split in two cohorts. We used microarrays to detail the global programme of gene expression underlying the tumor biology
DESIGN: Every cases has been histologically reviewed by the pathologist subgroup and classified according to the 2002 WHO classification using histology, immunohistochemistry and molecular genetic and cytogenetic when needed
RELATIONS: BioProject: https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/bioproject/PRJNA126435
