# Note, 2026-11-06: redrawing the main figures adds descriptive panels only

The nine main figures of the Nature Communications manuscript (v16) are being redrawn at a higher panel density and in a new
visual style. Written and deposited before any new panel is drawn.

What may appear in a new panel:
- values already computed by a registered round or by an earlier script, read from the existing result tables;
- counts, shares, medians, quantiles and histograms of those values, split by the variables already in those tables;
- a Pearson or Spearman coefficient between two columns of those tables, printed as a description, with n and without a P value.

What may not:
- a new statistical test, P value or confidence interval that no earlier script computed;
- a new pipeline run, a new resampling, a new decoy set, a new cohort, or a new cut-off chosen after looking at results;
- a change to any existing estimate. Every result number printed on a v16 sheet stays on the redrawn sheet.

A new panel is described in its legend as descriptive, with n and with how any printed number was computed. It adds no
hypothesis and changes no conclusion. Where a new panel disagrees with the text or a legend, the disagreement is reported to the
authors rather than resolved in the drawing. Round 15 results are not used in this redraw; v16 reports rounds up to 14.
